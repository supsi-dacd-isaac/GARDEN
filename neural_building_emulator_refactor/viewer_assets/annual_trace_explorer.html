About these results

Five held-out buildings, four neural models and four policy datasets. The selected checkpoints come from the completed 15-epoch comparison. The two LSTM results were reused by that comparison. LightGBM predicts flexibility KPIs and has no physical T/Q/P trajectories to display.

All saved samples are retained, without smoothing or re-emulation. These predictive bands have no RNS/RNS–ECC post-calibration. Shading represents the requested generation lockout, not a constraint that delivered room heat must be zero. Times follow the dataset's local standard time without daylight-saving conversion.

Power units: Qroom per zone floor area; Pel_SH per building heated floor area. Source and alignment audit ↗
